1. Executive Summary & Core Innovation

The Campus Emergency & Smart Patient Triage System unifies Campus Emergency Response with an automated, clinical-grade Patient Triage Engine. Engineered to eliminate latency in university crises, it enables immediate bystander stabilization, intelligent unit dispatch, spatial clustering, and acoustic radio telemetry.

🗺️ 1. 2D Spatial Heatmap

Live interactive campus grid with blinking emergency radar beacons & multi-incident cluster alerts.

📡 2. Offline SMS Fallback

Zero-network resilience synthesizing compressed cellular SMS telemetry strings in basements & workshops.

📻 3. Live Radio Dispatch

Acoustic siren + browser SpeechSynthesis voice announcements for hands-free situational awareness.

📈 4. Triage Audit Bar

Live Mean EDT tracking (<1.8m), severity breakdown ratio bar, and ISO-9001 compliance analytics.

4. 1-Tap Zero-Delay Emergency Trigger System

⚡ Cardiac / Collapse Protocol

Trigger: Unconscious, severe chest pain, cardiac arrest.
Units: Campus Medical Unit (CMU-1), Ambulance, Security Escort.
Bystander Action: CPR guidance (30 compressions, 2 breaths) & AED alert.

🩸 Severe Trauma & Bleeding

Trigger: Deep lacerations, blunt head trauma, heavy blood loss.
Units: CMU-1 Trauma Responder + First-Aid Mobile Unit.
Bystander Action: Direct firm pressure, no object removal, keep warm.

🔥 Active Fire & Smoke Hazard

Trigger: Electrical fire, toxic smoke, chemical explosion.
Units: Fire & Safety Unit (FSU), Evacuation Team, Municipal Fire.
Bystander Action: Pull fire alarm, stairwell evacuation, stay low under smoke.

🚨 Physical Threat & Attack

Trigger: Hostile individuals, physical assault, weapon threats.
Units: Security Response Team (SRT), Campus Police, Medical Standby.
Bystander Action: Secure shelter, barricade doors, Run-Hide-Fight protocol.

7. Offline-First Resilience & Cellular SMS Fallback

Zero-Network Graceful Degradation: When a user experiences mobile data loss in basements or workshops, the portal automatically formats a standardized cellular SMS telemetry string:
SOS|ID:INC-B7E9D1|LOC:Block-B-2nd-Floor|TYPE:Medical|AGE:21|PRI:EMERGENCY|SYM:Unconscious-bleeding|SEC:77491

Callers can launch their native SMS app with pre-filled toll-free shortcode (1800-CAM-911) or copy the payload, while receiving instantaneous local first-aid instructions without needing active server roundtrips.

8. Acoustic Siren & Live Synthetic Radio Dispatch

To ensure operators are immediately alerted without continuously staring at screens, the platform couples a Web Audio API oscillating siren with native browser speechSynthesis radio announcements:

"Attention. Code Red Emergency. Immediate dispatch Campus Medical Unit CMU-1 to Mechanical Workshop Ground Floor. Critical incident reported."

Includes walkie-talkie squelch audio bursts, speech rate optimization (1.05x), and an administrative Mute/Test toggle for live judging demonstrations.
